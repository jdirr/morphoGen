Files

Every model is described by implicit functions: the files follow its interfaces, not a voxel image. A morphology of several phases (coatings, inclusions, dead leaves, osteons, cells with phases) gives the function and the surface of each phase (level_set_phase1.vti, surface_phase1.stl…) and a mesh conforming to all their interfaces. Other formats (Gmsh, Abaqus, Neper, TIFF…): Solvers & formats.
